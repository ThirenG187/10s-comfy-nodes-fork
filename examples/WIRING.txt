T2V — BFS guided multi-face
===========================

Empty LTX Latent ------------------------------> target_latent
Group/composition image -----------------------> target_image
Subject 1 portrait ----------------------------> reference_image
Subject 2 portrait ----------------------------> subject_2_reference_image
Loaded LTX model + compatible identity LoRA ---> model
LTX VAE ---------------------------------------> vae

Settings:
  target_image_mode = assignment_and_bfs_reference
  assignment_mode = left_to_right
  reference_layout = overlap
  target_guide_strength = 0.35
  zero_reference_timesteps = true
  reference_guidance_scale = 1.0

The node's MODEL output goes to the sampler.
